Premium Coated Fries   

Elevate your menu with our coated, pre-fried, and quick-frozen French Fries, expertly crafted for ultimate convenience and taste. Perfect for busy kitchens and snack lovers, these fries deliver a crunchier exterior and a fluffy interior, making them ideal for fast food outlets, cafes, and restaurants.

Why Choose IFCG?
– Premium quality potatoes, carefully selected and processed.
– Produced under strict hygiene and food safety standards.
– Trusted supplier with strong experience in frozen food production and export.
– Consistent cut size and coating for longer hold time and better crispiness.

Product Benefits:
– Made from 100% premium potatoes
– No artificial preservatives or additives
– Coated for extra crunch and flavor
– Halal certified & vegan friendly
– Excellent yield and minimal oil absorption
– Easy to cook from frozen, perfect for high-volume operations

Shelf Life & Storage:
– Shelf Life: 18 months from production date
– Storage: Keep frozen at -18°C or below. Do not refreeze once thawed.
